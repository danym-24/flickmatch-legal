Frequently asked questions

How do I create a room?

From the home screen, choose whether you want to explore movies or TV shows, then tap "Create Room."

Next, share the room link with another person so they can join.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Profile tab and select "Delete Account."

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data and the information related to your rooms and games will be deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

If you have an active FlickMatch Plus subscription, cancel it first from your Apple account. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I cancel my FlickMatch Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account. Go to:

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FlickMatch Plus → Cancel Subscription

Canceling prevents future renewals, but it does not immediately end the period you have already paid for.

What's the difference between the free version and FlickMatch Plus?

The free version has usage limits. FlickMatch Plus expands those limits and lets you hand-pick the movies in your rooms' decks.

Features and limits may change with future app updates.

Where does the movie and TV show information come from?

From the TMDB (The Movie Database) API. FlickMatch uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
